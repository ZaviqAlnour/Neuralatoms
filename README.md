# NeuralAtoms (Ω)

**The Data Infrastructure for the Physical Intelligence Revolution.**

---

## Overview
NeuralAtoms (Ω) is a high-fidelity, physics-first data pipeline designed to bridge the **"Atoms Bottleneck."** By reconstructing the hidden physical variables of human movement and interaction, we provide the foundational intelligence required for the next generation of generalist robotics and autonomous systems.

## Key Capabilities
Our engine operates at the intersection of computer vision and classical mechanics, delivering standardized data across three critical dimensions:

- **Kinematics (K):** High-precision 3D motion reconstruction and world-grounded skeletal geometry.
- **Dynamics (D):** Deep-layer physical recovery, including Inverse Dynamics and Force/Torque estimation.
- **Context (C):** Semantic environmental mapping, friction coefficient recovery, and mass-interaction analysis.

## Output Format
NeuralAtoms produces standardized **.atoms** experience sets. These are high-density, time-aligned datasets optimized for training and deploying Physical AI in complex, real-world environments.
